APEX LEX GROUP WEBSITE
======================

Folder structure
  index.html            - all pages (Home, Branches, About Us, Privacy, Terms)
  assets/css/style.css  - colours, fonts, layout
  assets/js/main.js     - page switching, contact form, disclaimer
  assets/img/statue.png - Lady Justice image
  assets/img/favicon.svg- browser tab icon
  robots.txt, sitemap.xml - for Google

How to publish
  1. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder (index.html must be at the top level).
  2. Netlify: drag this folder to app.netlify.com/drop.
     Hostinger: File Manager > public_html > upload all files and folders.

Things you can change
  - Email and form service: top of assets/js/main.js (TO and ENDPOINT).
  - Colours: top of assets/css/style.css (:root).
  - Text: index.html.

After going live, send one test enquiry and click Activate in the
FormSubmit email received at the enquiry address.
