About Us

One name, three branches, one standard of work.

Apex Lex Group is a family of separate practices and businesses that share one name and one commitment: work done properly, in writing, and on time.

Land, construction and business problems rarely sit in one field. Having three disciplines under one group means a matter can be referred to the right team without starting again, while each team remains responsible for its own field.

Our mission

To provide dependable legal, construction and software services, each delivered by a qualified team that is accountable for its work.

Our vision

To grow as a group of professional practices guided by integrity, clarity and consistent standards.

Our values

Integrity

We state facts honestly and keep our commitments.

Confidentiality

Information entrusted to us is protected and used only for its stated purpose.

Accountability

Every branch answers for its own work, scope and timelines.

Clarity

We explain options and consequences in plain language.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Apex Lex Group and its branches (Apex Lex Associates, Apex Lex Builders and Apex Lex Software, together “we”) handle personal information received through this website. We process personal information in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws of India.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what you enter in the enquiry form:

  • Name and mobile number
  • State and district
  • The branch you wish to contact and, for legal enquiries, the type of matter
  • The message you write

We do not ask for Aadhaar, financial or other sensitive information. Please do not include such information, or confidential or privileged details of a legal matter, in the form.

2. Why we collect it

We use your information only to read and respond to your enquiry and to contact you about it. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. How your enquiry reaches us

When you submit the form, your message is delivered to our email address (teamapex173@gmail.com) through a third-party form delivery service. That service transmits the message to us on our behalf. Your email application may also be used to prepare the message if the form service is unavailable.

4. Consent

You give your consent by ticking the consent box in the form before sending it. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us at the email address below.

5. Storage and retention

Enquiries are kept in our email account and retained only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for our legitimate record-keeping, after which they are deleted. We take reasonable steps to keep this information secure, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s session storage only to remember, for the current visit, that you accepted the disclaimer shown on entry.

7. Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except the service providers needed to deliver your message to us, or where we are required to do so by law.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to erase it, and you may withdraw your consent. Write to teamapex173@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

This website is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. Please do not submit information about a child without the consent of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version, with its date, will always be on this page.

11. Contact

For any question or request about your personal information: teamapex173@gmail.com
